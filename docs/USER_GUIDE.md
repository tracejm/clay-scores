# Clay Scores – User Guide

Clay Scores keeps score for a squad of up to 5 shooters during a round of
sporting clays or 5-Stand. It works with no cell signal, and all data stays on your phone.

**App address:** https://tracejm.github.io/clay-scores/

## Install it

Open the app address once while you have a signal, then:

- **Android (Chrome or Brave):** ⋮ menu → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
- **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** **Share** button (square with ↑) → **Add to Home Screen**.

Always open it from the Home Screen icon. It updates itself when opened with a signal.

## Before your first round

1. **Manage Courses** → **+ Add New Course**. Enter the course name and location, and choose the
   **Course Type**: **Sporting Clays** (the default) or **5-Stand**.
2. **Manage Shooters** → **+ Add New Shooter**. Each name must be unique.

Tap any course or shooter in the list to fix a spelling. Rounds that already use it are updated too.
Changing a course's type only affects new rounds; rounds already created keep their layout.

## Shoot a round

1. **Create New Round.** Pick the course, check the date and time (defaults to now), and set the
   number of stations with **−** / **+**. Leave **Rotate shooters at each station** ticked to change who shoots first at every
   station, or untick it to keep the same order (A,B,C) all round. Choose up to 5 shooters. Only Shooter 1 is required.
   Tap **Save**.

   **5-Stand courses** are set up for you: always 5 stations of 5 targets, no rotation. These
   can't be changed, and you can start scoring straight away.
2. **The round screen** shows one tile per station (✎ marks stations with notes) and the current totals:
   targets, breakage, look and practice targets, and each shooter's hits. Tile borders show progress: **grey** = not started, **amber** = in progress, **green ✓** = everyone scored.
3. **Tap a station.** Set the number of targets with **−** / **+** (1–12, default 10) and tap the
   green **✓** to confirm. (5-Stand stations are fixed at 5.)
4. **Score each target** by tapping its circle:

   | Tap | Circle | Means |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1st | Green **+** | Hit |
   | 2nd | Red **−** | Miss |
   | 3rd | Grey number | Not recorded yet |

   Circles come in pairs: the top circle in each column is an odd-numbered target and the bottom one is even. Black circles aren't used at this station.
   Every tap is saved immediately. There's no Save button.
5. **Shooting order** is shown top to bottom. With rotation on it changes each station
   (A,B,C → B,C,A → C,A,B …). With rotation off it stays A,B,C.
6. **Not scored:** under the shooters, use **−** / **+** to count **Broken targets** (broke before
   they could be shot at), **Look targets** (shown before shooting) and **Practice targets**. These don't
   affect anyone's score or the target total.
7. **Notes:** type anything about the station in the **Notes** box (for example, "long crosser, second bird
   drops fast"). It saves as you type.
8. **Move around:** use **‹ Station / Station ›** at the bottom, the round-name button at the top to go back to all stations, or **Home**.

## Send the results

On the round screen:

- **Email Round Results** opens a new email with the scores filled in. Add the recipients and send.
  The top of the message shows the course type, whether shooters rotated, and the total breakage, look
  and practice targets (only those that aren't zero). Any station notes are listed at the end.
- **Share results…** sends the same text through Messages, WhatsApp, Gmail and other apps.
- **Show email text** displays the results with a **Copy text** button.

## Manage Existing Rounds

Rounds are listed newest first. **Open** continues scoring or sends the results again.
**Delete** asks you to confirm, then permanently removes that round and its scores.
Shooters and courses are kept.

## Good to know

- **Each phone keeps its own data.** Scores aren't shared between phones. Use email to share them.
- **Removing the app from your Home Screen, or clearing your browser's website data, erases all scores.**
- To check you have the latest version, look at the number at the bottom of the home screen.
